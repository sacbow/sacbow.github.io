I'm Hajime Ueda, a PhD candidate at the University of Tokyo under the supervision of [Prof. Masato Okada](https://mns.k.u-tokyo.ac.jp/home.html).  
I am also a research part-time student at [Adaptive Bayesian Intelligence Team](https://adaptive-bayesian.ai/) at RIKEN. 

My research focuses on Bayesian methods for large-scale problems. More specifically, I am interested in:

1. **Large-scale Bayesian inference** arising in
  - Scientific Imaging
  - Deep Learning

2. **Scalable inference algorithms** including
  - Variational approaches 
  - Divide-and-Conquer approaches (such as Expectation Propagation)

3. **Building general tools for Bayesian methods** such as
  - [gPIE](https://github.com/sacbow/gpie-inference)
